Quantum Computing & Linear Algebra

    These notes review the core concepts of linear algebra and build toward the mathematical framework used in quantum computing and quantum mechanics. The focus is on vector spaces, matrices, linear transformations, and their role in describing quantum systems.

    Systems of Linear Equations

    A linear equation in multiple variables represents a constraint of the form:

    a₁x₁ + a₂x₂ + … + aₙxₙ = b

    A system of such equations can be written compactly as:

    A x = b

    where A is a matrix, x is the vector of unknowns, and b is the output vector.

    Matrices

    A matrix is a rectangular arrangement of numbers used to represent linear transformations and systems of equations.

    A = [aᵢⱼ]

    Matrix multiplication is defined as:

    (AB)ᵢⱼ = Σₖ Aᵢₖ Bₖⱼ

    Key Properties

    • Addition is commutative and associative
    • Matrix multiplication is associative but not commutative
    • Identity and inverse matrices play a central role in transformations

    Solution of Linear Systems

    A system A x = b may be:

    • Consistent: at least one solution exists
    • Inconsistent: no solution exists

    A homogeneous system A x = 0 always has at least the trivial solution x = 0.

    Vectors

    In physics, vectors represent quantities with magnitude and direction. In mathematics, vectors are abstract elements of vector spaces.

    A system of equations can also be viewed geometrically as a linear combination:

    x a₁ + y a₂ = b

    meaning we express a vector as a combination of basis vectors.

    Vector Spaces

    A vector space consists of:

    • A set V
    • Vector addition
    • Scalar multiplication
    • A field F

    These structures form the foundation of linear algebra and quantum theory.

    Why This Matters in Quantum Computing

    Quantum states are represented as vectors in a Hilbert space, and quantum operations are represented as matrices acting on these vectors. This makes linear algebra the mathematical backbone of quantum computing.
